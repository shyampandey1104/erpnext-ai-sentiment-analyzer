# 😡➡️😊 ERPNext AI Customer Support Sentiment & Churn Risk Analyzer

[![Python 3.9+](https://img.shields.io/badge/python-3.9+-blue.svg)](https://www.python.org/downloads/)
[![ERPNext](https://img.shields.io/badge/ERPNext-Support%20CRM-blue.svg)](https://erpnext.com)
[![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-yellow.svg)](https://opensource.org/licenses/MIT)

An NLP-driven **Customer Sentiment & Churn Prediction Engine** for **ERPNext Issue & CRM DocTypes**. Analyzes incoming support messages, flags high customer frustration/dissatisfaction, auto-escalates ticket priorities to `Urgent`, and alerts account managers to prevent customer churn.

---

## ⚡ Highlights
- 🧠 **NLP Polarity Scoring**: Real-time evaluation of sentiment score (-1.0 to +1.0).
- 🚨 **Automated SLA Escalation**: Promotes angry customer tickets straight to `Urgent`.
- 📉 **Churn Risk Flagging**: Highlights high-risk accounts considering cancellation/refunds.
- 🧪 **Pytest Coverage**: Unit tests included.
